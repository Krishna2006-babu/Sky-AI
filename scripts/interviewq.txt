🧠 Interview Prep: The "How" (The Magic Trick)
If an interviewer asks: "Krishna, how does your AI detect acne?"
You don't say: "I used a library."
You say:

"I implemented HSV Color Thresholding. I converted the image from BGR to HSV color space because standard RGB is too sensitive to lighting changes. I then created a Binary Mask that isolates pixels with a Hue value between 0-10 (Red), allowing me to calculate the percentage of inflamed skin."
